**Daily Claims Briefing – Operations Team**

Yesterday the team processed 75 claims, paying out just over $22,000 to providers while members owe roughly $32,000 out of pocket — worth noting that member responsibility is running significantly higher than what we're paying, which may drive call volume today from members surprised by their bills. About a third of claims were fully approved, half were partially approved, and roughly 1 in 5 were denied outright. The biggest story in yesterday's volume is that more than 40% of all claims touched a deductible issue, meaning members simply haven't met their annual deductible yet — this is likely seasonal and expected this time of year, but staff should be prepared to walk members through how their deductible works and how much they have remaining. Among the denials, the most common reasons were services not covered under the member's plan, out-of-network provider visits, and procedures that required advance approval but didn't have it on file — that last category, missing prior authorizations, is one where a follow-up from the provider could potentially reverse the denial, so it may be worth flagging those six claims for a second look. No single unusual spike stands out, but the combination of high member cost-sharing and a dense denial queue suggests today could be a busy one for member and provider inquiries.
